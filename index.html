“From Our Family to Your Forest.”

Chambers Logging and Tree Service is a family owned business built on generations of hard work and respect for the land. From multi scale logging and commercial projects to residential tree removal, trimming, and land clearing through Issaquah Premier Tree Service, we bring the same care, experience and professionalism to every job.

We are a small but dedicated team known for safe and efficient work with honest results. Whether you're managing timber, protecting your property, or improving your property, your land is treated with the respect it deserves.

Chambers Logging and Tree Service proudly serves homeowners, landowners, and commercial clients throughout Western Washington, including Issaquah and surrounding areas.
